# Blueprint Autonomous Organization Guide

> **Retired, 2026-11-12.** The role registry that was here (47 Paperclip agent
> roles, departments, routines, graduation rules) describes the autonomous org
> of spring 2026 and the frames after it. It is now
> `docs/archive/2026-11-12-pre-packet/AUTONOMOUS_ORG.md`. Read it only for
> history. Do not use it to decide what to build, how to word anything, or who
> approves what.

## What is current

- **What Blueprint does:** the shared block of
  [`PLATFORM_CONTEXT.md`](./PLATFORM_CONTEXT.md). In short: a site films a job
  once; Blueprint turns it into a job packet that any robot deployment company
  can evaluate without visiting, sends it to deployment companies it has
  approved, and tells the site who will take the job and on what evidence.
  Today a deployment company receives a summary of a job only; a site's video
  and simulation stay private until the owner sets a new permission.
- **Rules for every agent, human or AI:** [`AGENTS.md`](./AGENTS.md). The only
  hard stops are privacy, rights and money. Don't make things up.
- **Who approves:** the founder (decision 2026-09-28). No Paperclip role
  approves anything. Every approach to an outside company needs the owner's
  go-ahead first.
- **Paperclip:** every role is retired. Each agent's instruction files in
  `ops/paperclip/blueprint-company/agents/` say "do nothing", and every routine
  in `.paperclip.yaml` is paused. What actually runs on the Paperclip host is
  not recorded in this repo.
