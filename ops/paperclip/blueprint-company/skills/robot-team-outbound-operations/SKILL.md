# Robot-Team Outbound Operations

Use this skill when Blueprint is preparing outbound toward robot-team buyers or adjacent technical prospects.

Primary users:

- `robot-team-growth-agent`
- `outbound-sales-agent`

## Goal

Create disciplined outbound that turns real demand signals into specific, respectful outreach tied to an exact-site workflow need.

## Outreach Rules

- Follow the canonical [first-contact outreach policy](../../../../../docs/outreach-first-touch-policy.md) and its five-rule plus workflow pre-send review.
- Start with a source-verified connection, introduction, or shared community where possible; membership implies no endorsement.
- Lead discovery with web research and verified business contact routes. Network mining is stopped; LinkedIn is optional role verification. Do not require exhaustive network search before legitimate cold outreach. Only claimed relationships need connection proof. First-contact identity framing is “I’m building Blueprint,” without academic-research or large-company implications.
- For cold contact, cite one specific public detail and explain relevance, then offer a small observation or task-specific research brief with clear limits.
- Ask exactly one easy, non-confidential question first. Do not default to a meeting, questionnaire, video, or upload.
- Leave deeper conversation to the recipient's choice; reject invented relationships, unsupported claims, confidential asks, or pressure.
- Apply the policy's discovery-to-outcome approach: research site/job/team jointly with site-led discovery and team feasibility in parallel; disclose Blueprint identity immediately; keep talking interest, evaluation participation, and deployment capacity separate. Offer readiness/learning distinct from a qualified match or fee, progress the job brief before footage/details, and require site permission before sharing with teams. Team configuration/support/timing must be confirmed before a match promise. Evaluation and physical-outcome learning need evidence and consent. Reject unverified Atlas/pipeline claims.
- prefer signal-rich, low-volume outreach over list volume
- require a real contact hypothesis before drafting
- require a specific reason this team should care now
- ask for one small next step, not a broad sales motion
- hand off to `buyer-solutions-agent` when interest becomes concrete

## Required Output

Always return:

- `Target account`
- `Why now`
- `Why this contact`
- `Relevant Blueprint angle`
- `First-touch draft`
- `Follow-up condition`
- `Handoff trigger`
- `Evidence and bounded value`
- `Site/job/team research`: sources for both sides, feasibility gaps, and separate talking/evaluation/capacity signals. Unknown remains unknown.
- `Five-rule and workflow review`: connection, evidence/relevance, bounded value, easy question, recipient choice, workflow; `pass`, `revise`, or `block` with source references. Do not treat a draft's self-review as approval.

## Do Not

- send generic SDR spam
- optimize for activity count over reply quality
- promise pricing, coverage, or capability
- run long automated sequences without new signal
