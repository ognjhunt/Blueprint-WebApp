Jobs
Any job someone does over and over, like moving boxes, loading a cart or sorting items.
Where
We take videos only from sites in the United States.
Your job packet
What's in it
Your video and job description, a first look, and a simulation of your site when ready.
First look
An estimate of which robots could fit, based on published specs and prior use.
Simulation
A simulation helps companies review your job. It does not certify performance or safety.
Deployment companies
Responses
A company can answer Interested, Not a fit or Need more details. Once the simulation can be downloaded, it can also answer Tested it: can do or Tested it: cannot yet. We can't promise any will say yes.
Introductions
Press Introduce us beside a company that answered Interested or Tested it: can do. We email the company your name, address and contact details, and email you theirs.
Setting it up
You agree any work and its terms with the company directly.
Your video
Who sees it
We share it, without its sound, with the deployment companies we approve, so they can decide whether they can do your job. Your name, address and contact details go to a company only if you ask for an introduction.
Sharing
Sending a job means we share it. You agree to that once, when you send. If your job page asks you to agree, we share the job only after you do.
Stopping
You can stop us using your video at any time, from your job page. Your job then leaves every company's view at once. We ask any company that saved a copy to delete it, but we cannot take that copy back.
