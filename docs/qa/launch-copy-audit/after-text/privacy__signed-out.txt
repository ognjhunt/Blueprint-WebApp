Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

This version replaced the previous version (effective November 17, 2026).

This policy explains how Blueprint Robotics, Inc. (“Blueprint”, “we”), 6801 Burnet Rd, Austin, TX 78757, handles personal information on tryblueprint.io, in the private job links we send and in Blueprint accounts. Blueprint is responsible for that information.

What we collect

What you give us: your name, work email, company, role and phone number; the site and job you describe; your answers to the job brief; account details; and messages you send us. If a site and a deployment company agree to share a physical pilot's outcomes with Blueprint, we may also receive its scope, milestones and operating measurements under those permissions. Deployment companies give us their company's name, a contact name, email and website, and their answers about jobs. Blueprint takes no payments during the beta; Stripe holds the records of payments made before it. What we collect automatically: your IP address, browser and device type, pages you visit, and errors the site runs into.

Site footage

When a site films its job, we receive the video and photos, when and where they were taken, and the phone's motion and depth data where available. A phone video can show people and can include sound, such as conversations nearby. We keep the original upload as received, including any sound, for the uses and retention periods in this policy. We remove the sound before any automated review of the footage, before sending it to an AI or 3D-processing provider, and before a deployment company sees it, and we do not analyze speech. We and our listed processing providers use the footage to draft the job brief, review the job, and rebuild the work area as a simulated scene. Starting a job means we share it with robot deployment companies we've approved, so they can judge it without a visit. We share your description of the job, your video without its sound, the simulation we build from it, what counts as the job done, and our first look at which robots fit. We do not remove people from job footage or blur them. Blueprint and the listed processing providers, World Labs included, receive the footage, with its sound removed, or frames from it as recorded, which can show people. For a job started under a version of this policy published before November 17, 2026, we still blur faces in the frames we send to World Labs to rebuild the work area. Companies see your video as you filmed it, without its sound. We ask sites to avoid screens and paperwork.

How we use it

To run the Service: to reply to you, set up your job or account, draft the job brief, rebuild scenes, share each job with the deployment companies we approve, make the introductions a site asks for, record physical-pilot outcomes only under the parties' agreed permissions, send the emails you need (such as a job update or a company's answer), keep the Service secure and prevent fraud, understand how the site is used so we can improve it, and meet our legal obligations. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your footage to train AI models without your written agreement. The only use that California law could treat as sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising is the optional marketing cookies described below, which are off unless you turn them on.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent you gave, including consent to use your footage. You can withdraw your job at any time. It then leaves every company's view at once, and we ask any company that saved a copy to delete it. We cannot take back a copy a company has already saved. You can also change your cookie choices at any time, and we treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a choice to keep analytics and marketing cookies off.

Site footage in detail
Your video
Your recording is used inside Blueprint to draft the job brief, review the job and rebuild the work area. It is shared, without its sound, only with the robot deployment companies we approve, and only so they can judge the job. A deployment company may use your job only to decide whether it can do it. It may not pass it on, use it to train software, or keep it after we ask it to delete it.
People in job footage
We do not remove people from job footage or blur them. Blueprint and the listed processing providers, World Labs included, receive the footage, with its sound removed, or frames from it as recorded, which can show people. For a job started under a version of this policy published before November 17, 2026, we still blur faces in the frames we send to World Labs to rebuild the work area. Companies see your video as you filmed it, without its sound. You must have authority to submit the footage, tell people in the work area that you are recording and sharing the video with Blueprint and with the robot deployment companies we approve, and obtain any permission the law requires from people who can be seen or heard.
Sound
Phone videos can record sound, including conversations; Blueprint's browser recorder films without it. We keep an uploaded original as received, remove the sound before any automated review, before sending footage to an AI or 3D-processing provider and before a deployment company sees it, and do not analyze speech. Avoid filming conversations, and turn sound off where your camera allows.
Screens and paperwork
Avoid them when filming where practical: companies see your video as you filmed it.
Location
Used to tie a result to the right site, and to keep restricted areas you mark out of the scene. A deployment company sees the country of the job, not the address you typed.
Deployment companies
Starting a job means we share it with robot deployment companies we've approved, so they can judge it without a visit. We share your description of the job, your video without its sound, the simulation we build from it, what counts as the job done, and our first look at which robots fit. We do not share your name, your street address or your contact details with a deployment company unless you ask for an introduction to it. A job started before 12 November 2026 is not shown to companies unless you accept the current statement on its page.
Withdrawal
You can withdraw your job at any time. It then leaves every company's view at once, and we ask any company that saved a copy to delete it. We cannot take back a copy a company has already saved.
Physical pilot records
If the site and the deployment company agree to share pilot outcomes with Blueprint, those permissions set what operating records we receive, who may see them, and the permitted uses. A simulation result does not become a physical result without actual trial evidence.
Deployment companies

Your account holds your company's name, the contact details you give us and your answers about jobs. A site sees your answer under an alias. It sees your company's name and your contact details only when it asks for an introduction after you answered Interested or Tested it: can do. When a site asks for an introduction, we give you the site's name, address and contact details. Results and records from before the free beta keep their original sharing restrictions.

Sites

Your job holds your contact details, the site's address, the job brief, your footage, the areas you mark as restricted, the answers deployment companies gave and the introductions you asked for.

Earlier filming visits

Video from an earlier Blueprint filming visit is handled like uploaded video.

Cookies and similar technologies

Essential cookies and browser storage keep you signed in, protect forms from forgery, remember your cookie choices and keep an unsent job draft on your device. They are always on, because the site does not work without them.

Analytics cookies, from Google Analytics and PostHog, tell us which pages are used so we can improve them. Marketing cookies let Google Analytics measure whether our outreach works. Both are off until you allow them, and neither is pre-selected. Google Analytics, PostHog and optional analytics events sent to our own servers stay off until you allow the corresponding category. Session recording is disabled. Necessary service and security requests still run. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we keep both off unless you turn them on yourself.

You can change your choice at any time: open cookie settings (also in the footer of every page).

Who we share it with

We share personal information with the providers below, only for the function listed and under contract. We also share each job with the robot deployment companies we approve. They receive it as the job’s recipients, not as our providers: the site’s description of the job, its video without its sound, the simulation, what counts as the job done and our first look, only so they can judge the job. A site’s name, address and contact details go to a company only when the site asks for an introduction to it, and the site then receives the company’s name and contact details. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect people’s safety or our rights, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply to it.

We send each provider only what the step needs, footage goes to them with its sound removed, the provider may not use it for its own purposes, and we do not give any of them your footage as training data.

How long we keep it

A job closes on the earliest of: the site owner asking us to close or delete the job; the site owner withdrawing permission to use the recording (for the footage, from that moment); or 12 months passing with no activity on the job from the site, a deployment company or Blueprint.

Site footage and capture data
Existing recordings keep their original retention terms; longer retention requires separate authorization. The video, photos, timestamps, motion and depth data, the frames and any blurred copies we make from them and store with them, and the record of the site's authority and privacy review. Recordings collected under the version of this policy effective October 13, 2026 keep its 180 days after the job closes schedule, subject to applicable legal holds. Publishing this update, reaching its effective date, or accepting it later does not extend that period for existing recordings. A proposed period of at least 7 years for newly collected recordings requires legal review and the notice and authorization applicable to those recordings before it can apply. Our storage does not currently delete originals automatically on a schedule; that configuration does not override the applicable retention terms. Withdrawal and verified deletion requests use our deletion process, after we confirm the request and check for any legal hold. When a site withdraws permission, we stop using the recording from that moment.
Temporary processing files
14 days after they are created. Scratch files from processing footage that sit in our temporary processing storage, unless a legal hold applies. Frames and any blurred copies stored with the capture are kept like the capture.
Scenes, evaluation results, pilot records and reports
365 days after the job closes or the contract ends. The rebuilt scene, the answers deployment companies gave, introductions and reports. Physical pilot measurements are included only under a separate agreement, which may set a different retention period and permitted use.
Account and contact details
While your account or job is open. Deleted or anonymized after you close your account, your job closes, or we complete a verified deletion request, unless a record below still needs them.
Support and privacy requests
90 days after the request is closed. The messages and the record of what we did.
Payment, tax, security and legal records
As long as the law requires. Includes records of payments made before the free beta. US tax records are typically kept for up to 7 years. Records under a legal hold, or needed to investigate fraud or a security incident, are kept until the matter is resolved.
Your rights and how to use them

To request deletion of your account and personal data, open account deletion in Settings. You can view your request reference and status there. Submitting the request starts our review; it does not mean your account is already closed or every copy is erased. We review site and company records, provider copies, backups and any required retention before confirming the outcome. You can also email us, including if you cannot sign in.

Email privacy@tryblueprint.io from the address on your account, or include enough detail for us to find your records and confirm they are yours. Tell us what you would like us to do.

We acknowledge your request within 10 business days and complete it within 30 calendar days of confirming your identity. If we need longer, we tell you why.

We may keep some information when the law requires it, or when it is needed for a payment record, a legal claim, fraud prevention or security. If so, we tell you what we kept and why.

We do not charge for a request, and we do not treat you differently for making one. An authorized agent can make a request for you with your written permission.

California privacy rights

This section applies to California residents under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act.

Selling
We do not sell personal information, and we have not sold it in the past 12 months. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.
Sharing for advertising
If you turn on marketing cookies, Google Analytics may use them to measure whether our outreach and ads work. California law can treat that as sharing personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. It happens only after you turn marketing cookies on, and you can turn them off at any time in cookie settings.
Global Privacy Control
If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as your request to opt out of sale and sharing: analytics and marketing cookies stay off and are not pre-selected. You can still turn them on yourself in cookie settings.
Sensitive information
We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you, and we use it only as needed to provide the Service.
Your rights
California residents can ask to know, access, correct or delete their personal information and to opt out of sale or sharing, as described above, without being treated differently for asking.
Where your information is processed
Where we operate
Blueprint's beta serves sites and deployment companies in the United States, and our providers process data mainly in the United States.
Outside the United States
If you contact us from outside the United States, your information is transferred to and processed in the United States. We work with a site or company outside the United States only under signed transfer terms, such as standard contractual clauses.
Provider limits
Each provider receives only what it needs for the function listed above, under contract, and may not use it for its own purposes. We do not send site footage to any provider as general training data.
Security

We encrypt information in transit and at rest, additionally encrypt contact details in our database, and limit access to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

When you submit a job or upload footage on our website, we use Firebase App Check with Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise to confirm the request comes from our site rather than an automated script. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information, such as device and browser data and your IP address, and sends it to Google, whose use of it is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We will post any update here with its effective date, and email account holders before a material change takes effect.
