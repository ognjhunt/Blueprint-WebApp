# Launch copy: decisions

What every customer-facing word on Blueprint follows, and the final change list from the audit of 2026-11-20. The evidence is in [inventory.md](inventory.md) (95 screens, desktop and phone), [emails.md](emails.md) (every email, rendered) and [findings.md](findings.md) (every verified finding, with the tests each one touches).

The bar: would a first-time site manager, or a deployment company's business-development lead, find this natural, necessary and obvious? A sentence that doesn't help the reader do the next thing goes.

## Voice rules

- Plain words a 10-year-old understands. One idea per sentence; about 20 words at most.
- Say what the person can do next. When nothing is needed, say "Nothing is needed from you."
- Show the viewer's own email only where they are confirming it ("We sent your link to {email}" right after they submit). Never as a "Signed in as" line.
- No internal words: staff view, scene, grant, evidence tier, brief, versions, schema or state names, provider names, IDs, internal dates. The one ID kept is the start-form failure reference, and the line says why it is shown.
- Nothing implies a person reviews a site's job or video ("our team reviews", "needs review"). No person stands between a video and an introduction.
- Nothing says Blueprint tests robots or runs robot software. Blueprint stopped running robot software in the simulation on 2026-11-14.
- Internal budgets are never mentioned to a customer: no "spending cap".
- "(optional)" is dropped from disclosure toggles and section headings. It stays only on a form field that sits beside required fields and doesn't already read as optional.
- "beta" only where it tells the reader something.
- Quote style is not changed for its own sake. New strings follow the file they live in. Recorded statements keep their exact characters.
- Privacy, sharing and withdrawal sentences may get shorter, never weaker. They still say what is shared, with whom, how to stop, and that a copy a company already saved is asked to be deleted but can't be taken back.

## Decisions made in synthesis

1. **One label for the company job list: Find jobs.** The two verifier groups split between "Find jobs" and "Browse jobs". "Find jobs" is already the header link and the /jobs page, so it wins everywhere: the `/contact/robot-team` heading and title (`BROWSE_JOBS_TITLE`), the nav, workspace links and the emails (e-010, e-030, f-102, v-B-001 adjusted).
2. **"Jobs that involve picking something up…" goes** (a-019, verifier overridden on the owner's example). How it works drops the line. The Questions "Jobs" row says something that helps: "Any job someone does over and over, like moving boxes, loading a cart or sorting items." (Blueprint accepts every job; owner 2026-11-17.)
3. **The staff-view line on the job list goes** (c-001). Staff see exactly what an approved company sees. No staff hint is added to the customer page.
4. **/onboarding is retired as a checklist** (d-058 to d-068). Every account without `finishedOnboarding` lands there, sites included, and it reads entirely in pipeline terms ("proof-ready intake", "buyer path", "review queue"). A site goes to its jobs (`/app`) and an approved company to Find jobs. If routing can't change safely, the page becomes one plain screen with one next step.
5. **The development consent boxes do render in production today.** Adding `?authoring=claude` or `?authoring=sol` to the public start form shows them (b-001; screenshot in the inventory). They now render only in development builds. The recorded text (2026-09-24.v1) is unchanged; only who can see it changes.
6. **Refunds leave the product copy** (a-011, a-012, a-048). The footer link, the "Refunds and cancellations" link beside "Free for sites" and the sitemap entry go. The page itself stays routed (legal, audit-only) until the owner confirms whether any outside customer bought a pre-beta balance.
7. **The share image is re-rendered** (a-001). Every shared link showed "Robot teams evaluate your task in simulation. We recommend the one that passed. Free during the beta." It now shows the home page's title and sub-line, plus "Free for sites."
8. **One way to say the answers.** In `publicCopy.ts`, `COMPANY_ANSWERS` becomes the three everyday answers, and a short second sentence adds the two "Tested it" answers once the simulation can be downloaded (c-025). Emails build their sentence from `COMPANY_RESPONSE_LABELS` directly.
9. **Introduction emails: fix the words, not the sending code** (e-001, e-002). "Reply to them directly" was untrue, because a reply goes to Blueprint. It now says to write to them at the address in the email. Whether introductions should carry a Reply-To is an owner question.
10. **Settings asks only what each account needs** (c-045, d-031 to d-038). The targeting preferences block (embodiments, policy categories, data retention, rights scopes) goes; every approved company is emailed each new job anyway. Sites don't see the job-email box.
11. **The job reference appears only when a start fails** (f-001, f-002, b-023, b-014). It reads "If this keeps happening, email hello@tryblueprint.io and include this reference: …".
12. **Where Blueprint works** (a-018, b-060, v-A-001): "We can only take videos from sites in the United States for now. You can save your job, but please don't film or send a video yet." This is true: a site outside the US can still save a job.

## Questions for the owner

1. **Refunds:** did any outside customer buy a pre-beta balance? If none, retire /refunds and the Terms refund clauses. If some did, keep a short unlinked page.
2. **Marketing cookies:** the banner says "Personalized ads and content", and the code does grant ad storage when marketing is on. The Privacy Policy says marketing cookies only measure outreach. One of them must change, and the banner matches the code today.
3. **Legal-change notice:** most sites have only a job link, not an account. Should notice of material changes also go to job contacts? Today nothing sends it.
4. **Footage retention period** for videos collected since 2026-10-13. The legal pages can't state one until scheduled deletion exists.
5. **`?authoring=` tests:** were they ever run on production for real sites? If yes, they now need a development deploy (decision 5).
6. **Introductions:** should introduction emails carry a Reply-To with the other side's address? Today a reply goes to Blueprint.
7. **The robot-fit hold** (`robot_fit_none`): is it still emitted now that no robot software runs? Its copy was fixed either way.
8. **Older screening and evaluation run records:** do any outside jobs still show them? Their legacy headlines were rewritten either way.
