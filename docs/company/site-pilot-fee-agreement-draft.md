## Fee

Blueprint may screen the task and invite suitable teams to evaluate it without an upfront charge. If the site purchases a physical pilot for this task from a provider Blueprint introduces within 12 months after that introduction, the site pays Blueprint 5% of the provider's physical pilot charges, capped at $5,000 in total for this task. The fee applies whether the site and provider contract or communicate through Blueprint or directly. The site may propose a pilot price and conditions, or state a target budget. Neither binds a provider or authorizes a purchase; the site and provider agree on final price and scope. The provider contracts for the physical work. Blueprint does not guarantee the pilot's result.

The fee is calculated on provider pilot charges actually paid by the site, excluding taxes, refunded amounts, and later ongoing deployments. Blueprint invoices the fee after the corresponding provider payment. No purchased pilot means no Blueprint fee. If provider charges are later refunded, Blueprint adjusts its fee accordingly. A later deployment or another task requires a separate agreement.
