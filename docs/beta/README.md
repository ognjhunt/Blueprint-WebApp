# Blueprint beta

This folder explains the invited beta as it works today. The frame is the
shared block of [`PLATFORM_CONTEXT.md`](../../PLATFORM_CONTEXT.md) (owner
direction 2026-11-12).

## How the beta works

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1. Email and video | A site sends an email address and a video of one recurring job. |
| 2. Job packet | Blueprint turns the video into a job packet: the site's sentence for the job, a first look on paper, a simulation of the site, and what counts as done. Where one exists, the result of our early trial of openly available robot software. |
| 3. Deployment companies | With the site's permission, Blueprint shares a summary of the job with deployment companies it has approved by hand, and records their responses. |
| 4. Where the job stands | The site's job page says plainly: not yet sent, sent and waiting, or the responses given. |

- Sites pay nothing. No price is stated anywhere.
- V1 is by hand. Every approach to an outside company needs the owner's
  go-ahead first.
- Today a deployment company receives a summary of a job only. A site's video
  and simulation stay private until the owner sets a new permission.
- Nothing promises a response that has not been given.
- The only hard stops are privacy, rights and money. Anything else that is
  missing is flagged, and the job continues.
- A simulation never certifies physical performance or safety.
