# Invited site beta

Start with one recurring job at [Discuss your site](https://tryblueprint.io/contact/site-operator). Describe where objects start, what happens, what success means, and what is difficult today. You can start without an account, a robot-team commitment, or footage. This is a founder-supported experimental assessment; a submission does not schedule a visit or promise a robot partner.

## Your next steps

1. Keep the private job link returned by the form. Review and correct the drafted brief, including open questions, success targets, and pilot preferences. “I don't know” is an acceptable answer; missing evidence stays visible.
2. Add existing video or record later if useful. Recording permission is separate from permission to describe the job. A description can still be reviewed while recording is on hold.
3. Follow the assessment's current next action. Saved video, accepted processing, scene availability, completed evaluation, and physical results are separate states. A realistic scene or simulated result does not certify physical performance or safety.
4. If you want a public job card, review and explicitly approve its text. You can decline listing. An account is offered when you save ownership and follow the job; the initial description does not require one. Robot-team beta access follows the existing approval flow and is free.
5. Blueprint presents one recommended pilot only when a suitable team and scoped proposal exist. Review what each party provides, cost, timing, and remaining uncertainty. Only **Book this pilot** authorizes the existing **$2,500 Blueprint fee at booking**; it is separate from the robot team's stated pilot cost. A booking record alone does not prove a payment succeeded. No pilot, no fee. Robot-team participation, site access, dates, and physical work require their own confirmation.

## Footage and permission

Only provide footage you are authorized to share and use for the stated purpose. Film the work and objects; avoid identifiable people, confidential screens, restricted areas, and private records. Review the [privacy policy](https://tryblueprint.io/privacy) and the permission statement before uploading. Do not put credentials or sensitive footage in support email. Keep your original file until receipt is confirmed.

The private owner link carries authority over the job; keep it private. Use the dedicated film-only link when asking an authorized colleague to record. Public listing approval does not make private footage public.

To withdraw recording permission or request handling/deletion review, stop sharing and contact **hello@tryblueprint.io** with the job reference and affected footage. Blueprint must confirm the affected use/access was stopped and report any pending downstream acknowledgment or expiring link. Withdrawal is not proof that every copy has already been deleted; do not upload again to clear a withdrawal hold.

## Recover without starting over

| What you see | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| Interrupted description save | Retry from the same open form. If receipt is uncertain after closing it, check your return email or contact support before creating a duplicate job. |
| Link cannot be checked or has expired | Reopen it; use **Email me a fresh link** if offered. A successful request is not proof of delivery. If it remains blocked, contact support. |
| Upload failed or status is unknown | Keep the original video. Reopen the job page to check receipt before selecting it again. |
| Video received; processing not confirmed | Use **Retry processing** only when offered. This reuses the saved video. Otherwise contact support with the displayed reason. |
| Consent or rights hold | Stop processing attempts. Resolve the named permission issue with the site owner/support. |
| Brief correction failed | Keep the page open and retry the correction. Reopen the job link to verify the saved answers. |
| Recommendation changed or booking acknowledgment interrupted | Reopen the job page to inspect the current recommendation and booking before retrying. Do not infer booking or payment from a missing response. |

## Founder follow-through

Email **hello@tryblueprint.io** with your job reference (avoid forwarding the full private token), the page/step, approximate time, expected outcome, and exact error. The founder owns triage until a named helper accepts it. For each invited site, the existing operating record should hold: **owner, current evidence/status, smallest missing input, next action, and the next promised update time**. The founder agrees that update time with the participant; this guide does not promise a response-time guarantee.

A useful follow-up names what was actually received, what remains uncertain, who acts next, and when to check again. If there is no corroborated willing partner or an evaluation is blocked, say so and continue bounded description review. A native evaluation dependency is not a prerequisite for an initial site conversation.
