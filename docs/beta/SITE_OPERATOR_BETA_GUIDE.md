# Site beta guide

The beta is free. To start, send an email address and a video of one
recurring job at [Submit a job](https://tryblueprint.io/contact/site-operator).
You do not need an account, a budget or a chosen robot. We take videos only
from sites in the United States for now.

## What happens

1. **Email and video.** Send a .mov or .mp4 of one full round of the job and
   the work area around it. Keep the private job link the form gives you; we
   also email it to you.
2. **Job packet.** Blueprint turns your video into a job packet: your sentence
   for the job, a first look on paper at which robots could fit, and a
   simulation of your site. Blueprint runs no robot software in the
   simulation.
3. **Deployment companies.** Your job goes to the deployment companies
   Blueprint has approved. Each can apply, answer "Not a fit", or ask for more
   details. When a company applies, press **Accept** beside it to meet it.

Your job page says where the job stands, for example "Your job is ready for
deployment companies, but no approved company can see it yet.", and shows each
response. We can't promise any company will respond or say yes. There may be
no approved company that can see your job yet; your job page says so when that
is the case.

Blueprint does not refuse a job because of its kind. If something is missing,
your job page says so and the job keeps going.

A simulation is not a measurement of your real site. It never certifies
physical performance or safety. Any work is agreed between you and the
deployment company. Blueprint charges sites nothing.

## What to film

- Use a phone. Send a .mov or .mp4 file. Keep it under 1 GB if you can: files
  up to 2 GB are accepted, but a larger file may not be read, and your job
  page then asks for a smaller one.
- Film one full round of the job, from start to finish, in one shot.
- Hold the phone sideways if you can.
- Walk slowly in a half circle around where the job happens, and keep it in
  view.
- Keep the object the job moves in view the whole time.
- Use good light and a steady hand. Keep it under five minutes.
- Tell people nearby that the video goes to Blueprint and to deployment
  companies we approve. Avoid conversations: we remove the sound.
- Keep faces, screens and paperwork out of the shot. We do not blur the video.
- Film only where you are allowed to film and share.

## What you get, and when

| What | When |
|---|---|
| Your private job page, and an email with its link | A few minutes after you press Send video |
| The first look, on your job page | About a minute and a half in the one run we timed; not yet measured on a real site's job |
| The simulation of your site | Not yet measured on a real site's job. On our own trial jobs it took from about 10 hours to 7 days |
| Responses from deployment companies | Unknown. None is promised |
| Questions about what happened after you meet a company | 14, 45 and 90 days after the introduction |

Many simulations built so far have been the room only: nothing in it is set up
as a separate object a robot can move. Your job page says what yours holds,
including whether it has a rule for what counts as done; nothing more is needed
from you, and your job stays posted.

Paid work on your job stops 7 days after it starts. The job stays posted.

## What a deployment company sees

When you press **Send video** you agree that Blueprint shares your job with the
deployment companies it has approved: your description, your video without its
sound, the simulation, what counts as done, and your city and state. There is
no share button and no setting. Companies are approved by a published rule that
is checked automatically, or by Blueprint with one press.

Companies do not see the first look; it stays with you. They do not see your
name, street address or contact details. When you press **Accept** beside a
company, Blueprint gives it your email address and the place you gave us, and
gives you its details.

You can change your description from your job page. You cannot hide the job;
you can only stop it (below).

## Footage and permission

Only send footage you are allowed to share. Get any permission the law
requires before you film.

What Blueprint does with your video:

- Blueprint keeps your original upload as received. It removes the sound
  before any automated review, and before it sends footage to an AI or
  3D-processing provider. It does not analyze speech.
- Blueprint does not blur your footage or hold it because people appear in it.
  Blueprint relies on your consent.
- Read the [privacy policy](https://tryblueprint.io/privacy) before you upload.
  Do not put credentials or sensitive footage in a support email. Keep your
  original file until your job page confirms it arrived.

The private job link gives control of the job. Keep it private. Your video
without its sound goes to approved deployment companies only. It is not
public.

## Stop using my video

On your job page, press **Stop using my video**, then **Yes, stop using my
video**. Your job leaves every company's view at once, no new uploads are
accepted, and each company it was sent to is asked to delete any copy it saved.
A copy a company already saved cannot be taken back. The website deletes its
own stored copy. The copy on Blueprint's processing host is kept for now and
goes through Blueprint's deletion process, as the Privacy Policy describes.

Stopping is not deletion. As the page says: "We keep a copy of the video and
any results already made, as our Privacy Policy describes." To post the job
again you start a new job. For a deletion request, email
**hello@tryblueprint.io** with your job reference.

## Recover a saved job

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The description save was interrupted | Retry from the same open form. If you closed it, check your email or contact support before you start a duplicate job. |
| The link cannot be checked or has expired | Reopen it. Use **Email me a new link** if the page offers it. If it stays blocked, contact support. |
| The video is too large to send | Film a shorter clip, or record at 1080p, and send that from your job page. |
| The upload failed, or its status is unknown | Keep the original video. Reopen the job page to check it arrived before you select the video again. |
| The video arrived, but processing is not confirmed | Use **Start processing again** only when the page offers it. It reuses the saved video. Otherwise, contact support with the reason the page shows. |
| You need to send a different video | Use **Send a new video** on your job page. Your job and first video stay saved. |
| A consent or rights hold | Stop processing attempts. Resolve the permission issue with support. |
| A brief correction failed | Keep the page open and retry. Reopen the job link to check the saved answers. |

## Support

Email **hello@tryblueprint.io** with your job reference, the page or step, the
approximate time, what you expected and the exact error. Do not forward the
full private link. This guide does not promise a response time.
