Task assessment audit — 7 October 2026

The existing process produces a partial assessment. It has video observations, operator-confirmed brief fields, capability-band matching and deeper task-object preparation. It does not assemble the requested six-part, evidence-backed assessment, and the decision model does not receive the richer robot/history corpus. Gemini can support that assessment with a different prompt and connected knowledge tools; choosing regular or agentic video does not itself solve those gaps.

This report records the baseline before the newly requested agent implementation. No private video was opened, paid model evaluation run, live settings changed, or production service deployed during the audit.

Pantheon compares five models on 193 episodes/186 minutes with matched prompts, frames and output budget; apparent outcome agreement is evaluated on 171 teleop/UMI episodes. Sol 6.1 agrees with Astra on 95% and labels about 43 versus 44 events/minute. These measure agreement and annotation density, not human-ground-truth accuracy or site feasibility. Reported footage costs are $5/hour for Sol 6.1 versus $27 for Astra. Gemini 3.8 is absent, and no matched latency result establishes superiority here. [Pantheon study](https://pantheon.inc/research/argus)

Upstream is sampled frames plus setup-specific instructions/state sidecars: teleop 1.5s, UMI 1s, ego 0.5s; setup-dependent grids/resolution and teleop fine-detail routing. Recordings over 450 seconds are split/stiched. OpenRouter billed costs and direct OpenAI list-price estimates have different accounting. These inputs differ from Blueprint's native video, requested 2 FPS, narrow prompt and short clip. [Pinned upstream README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Pantheon-Industries-Inc/argus/6c99686a3d93027c517f02f37f24b5b68532e12a/README.md)

Static is suitable for short latency-sensitive clips; agentic navigation helps longer/selective queries and may add short-clip latency. **Recommended agent and minimum remaining gaps**

One assessment owner receives operator conversation and site identity, retrieves source-backed robot knowledge, calls Gemini with specific visual questions, asks the operator questions that change the recommendation, and emits the six-part concise assessment. Gemini supplies observations with timestamps/uncertainty. The owner may revisit Gemini to resolve ambiguity, but should ask humans for unobservable forces, acceptance targets, costs or constraints. Keep those evidence types separate throughout.

GPT-6.1 Sol at medium reasoning is a reasonable starting assessment model. OpenAI's professional/tool-work evidence motivates that choice; it is not a robotics-assessment validation. Keep the model replaceable and compare against Gemini as the assessment owner before declaring a winner. [Official Sol documentation](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-sol/)

Minimum gaps: preserve source-bound observations through final output; bind success to confirmed operator answers; retrieve robot constraints and exact supporting records with source dates; keep unknown capability distinct from exclusion; assess simple process/fixture/manual alternatives; return the smallest useful action; and retain one real end-to-end receipt. The new agent should return portable assessment, source catalog and tool receipts to the existing workflow owner, rather than introduce a second canonical datastore or autonomous deployment verdict.

**Bounded A/B proposal — not run**

Use 12–24 admitted, representative site bundles spanning short/medium/long video, occlusion, fast contacts, repeated/partial cycles, task variations, success then undone, wrong-job footage and cases where no robot is sensible. Include the dishwasher distinction as a regression case. Two independent human reviewers label observations, timestamp intervals, operator acceptance, source-supported constraints and missing information; adjudicate disagreements.

First compare current versus richer Gemini prompts with identical model/media inputs. Then compare owner models with identical video findings, approved KB snapshot, retrieval access and output contract. Separately compare native video against Argus-style frame annotation under a stated frame/token or dollar budget; do not change model and harness together and call the difference a model result.

Record source hashes, served model versions, prompts, image count/resolution, duration, FPS/requested versus retained, audio availability, reasoning/output budgets, query/filter/page/source logs, retry counts and actual cost accounting. Score event/object/timestamp precision and recall, unsupported claims, missed unknowns, false exclusions/qualification, success-criteria completeness, source coverage and usefulness of next action/no-robot recommendation. Measure whole-path p50/p95 latency and cost including decode, retrieval, thinking and retries. Repeat ambiguous cases to expose instability. Start any future paid run only under the owner's explicit bounded execution authority; no such run occurred in this audit.
