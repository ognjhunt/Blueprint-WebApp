# Capturer Beta Guide — retired

**Retired on 2026-10-13.** Blueprint no longer runs a capturer network. Since 2026-11-11 a
person who works at the site films the job on their own phone and uploads it in the browser;
no capture app is in use and Blueprint filming visits are retired too.

- The public guide at `/beta/capturer-guide` now redirects (301) to
  [Show us a task](https://tryblueprint.io/contact/site-operator).
- Sites: read [SITE_OPERATOR_BETA_GUIDE.md](./SITE_OPERATOR_BETA_GUIDE.md).
- Robot teams: read [BUYER_BETA_GUIDE.md](./BUYER_BETA_GUIDE.md).

Video from an earlier Blueprint filming visit is handled like uploaded video, as the
[privacy policy](https://tryblueprint.io/privacy) describes.

The previous guide's content (capturer payouts, assignments and review states) is in this
file's git history.
