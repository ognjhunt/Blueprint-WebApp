# Refunds and cancellations — review draft

Prepared October 19, 2026. **Draft for founder and counsel review.** The proposed
public page at `/refunds` restates existing terms and does not introduce a paid
offer. This document proposes no new refund amount, deadline, exclusion or
retroactive condition.

## The free beta

Blueprint's beta is free for sites and invited robot teams. Submitting a job,
screening, invited evaluations, a recommendation and booking the recommended
pilot carry no Blueprint fee. There are no paid private evaluations, top-ups or
checkout during the beta. As a result, there is no new Blueprint fee to refund.

## Purchases before the free beta

You can request a refund of an unused balance purchased before the free beta.
The refund and settlement terms applicable to that purchase continue to govern;
this page does not replace them. If Blueprint ends the Service for a reason
other than your breach of its Terms, Blueprint refunds any unused balance bought
before the free beta.

Email [hello@tryblueprint.io](mailto:hello@tryblueprint.io) from your account
email and include the purchase or receipt reference, if available, and the issue
you want reviewed. Never email a full card number, password or API key. We will
review the purchase and explain the outcome and any confirmed refund's expected
processing time. Submission of a request does not itself mean a refund has been
issued. Payment-provider/bank processing is separate from our review.

## Physical pilots

The site and robot team agree the physical pilot's scope, timing, price and
cancellation/refund terms directly before physical work. Blueprint does not
charge for that work or take a percentage. Contact the party you paid about a
refund; Blueprint cannot refund funds paid directly to a robot team.

If a recommended team withdraws or materially changes its confirmed scope
before the pilot starts, Blueprint seeks a replacement whose evaluation on the
same job passed, if one fits. Since the beta has no Blueprint fee, there is no
fee to refund. Contact hello@tryblueprint.io with your job link to discuss
replacement or cancellation. We do not guarantee that a replacement is available.

## Placement and release review

The `/refunds` page is linked beside **What does it cost?** on `/beta` and from
the public footer. The application has no beta checkout to place it beside.
Before any future paid product is authorized, the purchase surface must disclose
the specific price, currency, service scope, cancellation/refund terms and
contact route beside the final payment action; the selected Stripe payment
surface must carry the applicable terms as well. This is a future release gate,
not permission to reactivate payment routes now.

Review receipt coverage, historic purchase terms, refund operations, contact
ownership and mandatory local rights before adopting a new policy. Do not invent
a blanket "no refunds" clause or promise a processing time without evidence.

Reference: [Stripe website checklist](https://docs.stripe.com/get-started/checklist/website)
(reviewed October 19, 2026). Stripe's checklist calls for clear fulfillment and
contact information; publication alone does not establish account approval.
