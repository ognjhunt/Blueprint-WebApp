## 5. Free beta, physical pilots and refunds

and booking a recommended pilot are free during the beta. There are no new

The site and robot team agree the physical pilot's scope, dates, price,
cancellation terms and physical work directly. The team or integrator is
responsible for installation and operation. The site and responsible delivery
parties approve the safety plan. A booking does not reserve a date or substitute
for that agreement. Sharing physical-pilot outcomes with Blueprint requires the
parties' agreed permissions.

If a recommended team withdraws or materially changes its confirmed scope
before the pilot starts, Blueprint seeks a replacement whose evaluation on the
same job passed, if one fits. There is no new Blueprint fee to refund. Unused
balances bought before the free beta may be submitted for a refund under their
original purchase/refund terms by emailing hello@tryblueprint.io from the account
