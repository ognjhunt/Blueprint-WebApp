# Bounded automatic first contact

Blueprint may introduce itself to a verified public business contact at a
source-supported US site with one relevant, non-confidential question. The
message uses quality-reviewed public evidence and gives the recipient an easy
choice about whether to continue. Unknown interest stays unknown.

The question can reflect verified expressed interest, an existing pilot or
deployment only when the evidence supports that specific state. Generic
robotics activity, vendor speculation and future plans do not establish it.
Company headquarters alone do not establish the target site’s country.

This category covers a first introduction only. Follow-ups, replies, commercial
commitments, sensitive information and other outbound categories retain their
separate review requirements. It does not imply team participation, readiness,
pilot capacity, buying interest or a guaranteed match.

Messages identify their commercial purpose and provide a direct opt-out from
marketing mail. Suppression and previous contact are checked before sending.
An uncertain send acknowledgement must be reconciled rather than sent again.
A send receipt establishes acceptance by the mail provider, not inbox delivery.

Research discovery, quality review, publication and communication are distinct
steps. Completing or installing source code does not establish a live outcome.
